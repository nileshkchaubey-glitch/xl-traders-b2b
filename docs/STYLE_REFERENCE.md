# Storefront composition reference

Current design rules: [DESIGN_SYSTEM](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) and
[STOREFRONT_RULES](STOREFRONT_RULES.md). Current code establishes shipped state.
The original reference-app study, old phase queue and dated measurements are
preserved in archive; they are not a new implementation order.

Render the frozen design-reference prototype when comparing geometry. Preserve
XL Traders tokens, hierarchy, mobile density and reusable controls. Do not copy
another company's branding, photos or business claims. The prototype cannot
settle freight, stock, discount, pricing, catalogue or delivery policies.

Current Home composition and deliberate PDP/cart deviations are recorded in
DESIGN_SYSTEM and the dated PDP/cart report. Reuse ProductCard, PriceSlot,
ProductImage, ProductMeta and QuantityStepper. Compare guest and signed-in
rendering, focus, touch targets and horizontal overflow at mobile/desktop widths.
The latest owner launch sequence takes precedence over the old PR-0/A-1 plan.
